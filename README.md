## Overview

vernalAI is an original research and engineering project exploring a non-standard path toward artificial general intelligence.

Rather than scaling large static neural networks, vernalAI investigates *growth-based intelligence* — systems that begin simple and self-organize, adapt, and stabilize over time through local learning, memory consolidation, forward simulation, and internal self-modeling.

This repository represents an ongoing, phased construction of that system.

> **vernalAI is not a model.  
> It is not a framework.  
> It is not a reimplementation of existing architectures.**
>
> **It is a proto-brain.**

---

## Core Philosophy

vernalAI is built on several guiding principles:

- **Local learning** over global gradients
- **Prediction error and surprise** as primary learning signals
- **Sparse, conditional computation** for efficiency and stability
- **Episodic memory** with mistake-tagged replay
- **Imagination** via internal forward models
- **Identity stability** across continuous self-modification

The system is designed to *grow*, not merely train.

---

## Architectural Structure (High Level)

vernalAI is developed in explicit phases, each introducing a new cognitive capability while preserving stability of earlier phases.

High-level phases include (names simplified):

1. **Proto-Neurons & Life Loop**
2. **Plasticity & Gated Learning**
3. **Cortical Columns & Local Competition**
4. **Temporal Buffering & Planning**
5. **Episodic Memory**
6. **Forward Simulation (Internal World Model)**
7. **Goal Encoding & Self-Consistency**
8. **Value Stability & Drift Detection**
9. **Introspection & Self-Model**
10. **Identity Persistence**

Each phase is designed, implemented, tested, and refactored before progressing.

**Later phases do not overwrite earlier intelligence — they must coexist.**
